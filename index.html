About Us

PocketPhysiatrist is a medical reference app designed specifically for students, residents, physicians, and other clinicians in the Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation space. Access evidence-based clinical guides and assessment tools instantly on your iOS device.

Key Features

📚

Comprehensive Guides

In-depth clinical guides covering all major PM&R conditions and specialties, from spinal cord injury to pediatric rehabilitation.

✓

Assessment Tools

Quick access to validated assessment scales, examination techniques, and functional evaluation tools used in daily practice.

🔍

Smart Search

Find exactly what you need with intelligent search across all content, including real-time suggestions and keyword matching.

❤️

Favorites

Save your most-used references for instant access. Your favorites persist across sessions for a personalized experience.

🎨

Native iOS Design

Built with SwiftUI following Apple's Human Interface Guidelines for a familiar, intuitive experience that feels right at home on iOS.

📱

Works Offline

All content is available offline, ensuring you have access to critical information whenever you need it, even without internet.

Privacy Policy

PocketPhysiatrist is designed with your privacy in mind. We believe medical professionals should be able to access reference materials without concerns about data collection or privacy.

✓

Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. PocketPhysiatrist operates entirely on your device without requiring any personal data, account creation, or internet connectivity for core functionality.

Here's how we protect your privacy:

💾

Local Storage Only

All app data, including your favorites and preferences, is stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

📊

No Analytics or Tracking

PocketPhysiatrist does not use any analytics services, tracking codes, or third-party SDKs that could compromise your privacy. Your usage remains completely private.

🚫

No Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs that could access your information. Everything works offline and independently.

🔄

Policy Updates

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
