PocketPhysiatrist

Your comprehensive PM&R reference, always at hand

About Us

PocketPhysiatrist is a medical reference app designed specifically for students, residents, physicians, and other clinicians in the Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation space. Access evidence-based clinical guides and assessment tools instantly on your iPhone.

Key Features

📚

Comprehensive Guides

In-depth clinical guides covering all major PM&R conditions and specialties, from spinal cord injury to pediatric rehabilitation.

✓

Assessment Tools

Quick access to validated assessment scales, examination techniques, and functional evaluation tools used in daily practice.

🔍

Smart Search

Find exactly what you need with intelligent search across all content, including real-time suggestions and keyword matching.

❤️

Favorites

Save your most-used references for instant access. Your favorites persist across sessions for a personalized experience.

🎨

Native iOS Design

Built with SwiftUI following Apple's Human Interface Guidelines for a familiar, intuitive experience that feels right at home on iOS.

📱

Works Offline

All content is available offline, ensuring you have access to critical information whenever you need it, even without internet.
